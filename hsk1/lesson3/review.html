Lesson 3
Wǒ shì Zhōngguó rén
我是中国人
I'm Chinese — Full Review & Quiz
10-Question Review Quiz (复习测验)
1. How do you say "I am French" in Chinese?
  • A. 我是泰国人。(Wǒ shì Tàiguó rén.)
  • B. 我是法国人。(Wǒ shì Fǎguó rén.)
  • C. 我是中国人。(Wǒ shì Zhōngguó rén.)
  • D. 我是中文老师。(Wǒ shì Zhōngwén lǎoshī.)
Correct Answer: B. 我是法国人。(Wǒ shì Fǎguó rén.) "法国" (Fǎguó) means France and "人" (rén) means person, making "法国人" French person/nationality.
2. What function does the particle "的" (de) serve in "白家月的中文老师"?
  • A. Indicates a question
  • B. Expresses a possessive/attributive relationship
  • C. Negates the verb
  • D. Indicates past tense
Correct Answer: B. Expresses a possessive/attributive relationship. As taught in Xiaoyu's Classroom, "的" is placed between an attributive and a head noun to show possession ("Bai Jiayue's Chinese teacher").
3. When can the structural particle "的" (de) be omitted?
  • A. Before any verb or adjective
  • B. When preceded by a personal pronoun and followed by a kinship term or person noun (e.g., 我老师, 我妈妈)
  • C. Only at the end of a sentence
  • D. "的" can never be omitted under any circumstance
Correct Answer: B. When preceded by a personal pronoun and followed by a kinship term or person noun. For instance, saying "我老师" (my teacher) or "我妈妈" (my mother) without "的" is common and natural.
4. How do you ask "What nationality is your girlfriend?" in Chinese?
  • A. 你女朋友是谁？(Nǐ nǚpéngyou shì shéi?)
  • B. 你女朋友是哪国人？(Nǐ nǚpéngyou shì nǎ guó rén?)
  • C. 这是你女朋友吗？(Zhè shì nǐ nǚpéngyou ma?)
  • D. 你女朋友叫什么名字？(Nǐ nǚpéngyou jiào shénme míngzi?)
Correct Answer: B. 你女朋友是哪国人？ "哪国人" (which country person/nationality) is the standard question structure for asking someone's nationality.
5. Besides "shéi", how else can the character "谁" (who) be pronounced?
  • A. shí
  • B. shuí
  • C. suí
  • D. shè
Correct Answer: B. shuí. As noted in Xiaoyu's Tip in Text 2, "谁" has two standard readings: "shéi" and "shuí".
6. Which character transforms a statement into a yes-no question when added to the end of a sentence?
  • A. 的 (de)
  • B. 也 (yě)
  • C. 吗 (ma)
  • D. 太 (tài)
Correct Answer: C. 吗 (ma). Placing the modal particle "吗" at the end turns a statement into a question (e.g., 你忙吗？ = Are you busy?).
7. How do you say "I am not too busy" in Chinese?
  • A. 我不太忙。(Wǒ bú tài máng.)
  • B. 我很忙。(Wǒ hěn máng.)
  • C. 我不忙吗。(Wǒ bù máng ma.)
  • D. 我还忙。(Wǒ hái máng.)
Correct Answer: A. 我不太忙。(Wǒ bú tài máng.) "不太" means "not too" or "not very", so "不太忙" translates to "not too busy".
8. What is the correct English translation for "我们很想你" (Wǒmen hěn xiǎng nǐ)?
  • A. We are very busy with you.
  • B. We miss you very much.
  • C. They like your work.
  • D. You think we are good.
Correct Answer: B. We miss you very much. "我们" = we, "很" = very, "想" = miss, "你" = you.
9. What does the interjection "喂" (wèi) mean when answering a phone call or video call?
  • A. Hello / Hey
  • B. Goodbye
  • C. Thank you
  • D. Sorry
Correct Answer: A. Hello / Hey. "喂" (wèi) is the standard Chinese greeting used on the phone or during video calls.
10. What numbers are featured in the Lesson 3 tongue twister: "四是四，十是十。十四是十四，四十是四十"?
  • A. 3 (sān) and 13 (shísān)
  • B. 4 (sì), 10 (shí), 14 (shísì), and 40 (sìshí)
  • C. 7 (qī) and 17 (shíqī)
  • D. 1 (yī) and 100 (bǎi)
Correct Answer: B. 4 (sì), 10 (shí), 14 (shísì), and 40 (sìshí). This classic tongue twister practices distinguishing the 's' and 'sh' sounds using 4, 10, 14, and 40.
