Lesson 3
Wǒ shì Zhōngguó rén

I'm Chinese
🎯 目标 Objectives
  • 能听懂并使用“是”字句表达等同或类属。 Be able to understand and use the “是” sentence to express equivalence or categorization.
  • 掌握结构助词“的”的用法。 Master the use of the structural particle “的”.
  • 掌握用“吗”的是非问句的用法。 Master the use of the yes-no question with “吗”.

小语讲堂 Xiaoyu's Classroom
1 “是”字句 “是” Sentence

表示人或事物等同什么或类属什么，否定形式是“不是”。
The “是” sentence is used to indicate what somebody or something equals or belongs to. The negative form is “不是”.

🟥 大声朗读。 Read aloud.

(1) 我是法国人。

(2) 她是中文老师。

(3) 我老师不是法国人。

2 结构助词“的” Structural Particle “的”

结构助词“的”在定语和中心语之间，表达领属关系。
The structural particle “的” is placed between the attributive and the head noun/nominal phrase to express a possessive relationship.

🟥 大声朗读。 Read aloud.

(1) 白家月的中文老师

(2) 你的名字

如果“的”前边是人称代词，后边是亲属称谓或指人的名词，“的”可省略。
If the word “的” is preceded by a personal pronoun and followed by a kinship term or a noun referring to a person, “的” can be omitted.
🟥 大声朗读。 Read aloud.

(1) 我老师

(2) 我学生

(3) 你同学

(4) 我妈妈 (māma, mother)

3 用“吗”的是非问句 Yes-No Question with “吗”

“吗”是语气助词，通常在句子末尾，表示疑问。基本结构：……吗？
The word “吗” is a modal particle typically placed at the end of a sentence to indicate a yes-no question with “吗”. Basic structure: ...吗?

🟥 大声朗读。 Read aloud.

(1) 你也很忙吗？

(2) 你是他的中文老师吗？

(3) 你有 (yǒu, have) 姐姐吗？
