Hello, AI Xiaoyu! — Full Review & Quiz
10-Question Review Quiz (复习测验)
1. How do you say "Hello" in standard Chinese?
  • A. 谢谢 (xièxie)
  • B. 你好 (nǐ hǎo)
  • C. 再见 (zàijiàn)
  • D. 不客气 (bú kèqi)
Correct Answer: B. 你好 (nǐ hǎo). This is the standard greeting used for "Hello".
2. What is the respectful / honorific form of "you" used when addressing elders or teachers?
  • A. 大家 (dàjiā)
  • B. 你们 (nǐmen)
  • C. 您 (nín)
  • D. 同学 (tóngxué)
Correct Answer: C. 您 (nín). "您" is used to show respect to elders or teachers instead of "你".
3. Which suffix is added to personal pronouns or nouns to make them plural?
  • A. 们 (men)
  • B. 好 (hǎo)
  • C. 师 (shī)
  • D. 见 (jiàn)
Correct Answer: A. 们 (men). For example: 你 (you) -> 你们 (you all), 同学 (classmate) -> 同学们 (classmates).
4. What does "大家" (dàjiā) mean?
  • A. Teacher
  • B. Student
  • C. Everybody / Everyone
  • D. AI Assistant
Correct Answer: C. Everybody / Everyone. "大家好" means "Hello everyone!".
5. How should students address teacher "Ms. Wang" politely when greeting her?
  • A. 王同学，你好！
  • B. 王老师，您好！
  • C. 小语，你好！
  • D. 大家，再见！
Correct Answer: B. 王老师，您好！ In Chinese, family name comes first (王) + title (老师), followed by respectful greeting (您好).
6. What is the correct response when someone tells you "谢谢" (xièxie)?
  • A. 你好 (nǐ hǎo)
  • B. 再见 (zàijiàn)
  • C. 不客气 (bú kèqi)
  • D. 老师好 (lǎoshī hǎo)
Correct Answer: C. 不客气 (bú kèqi). "不客气" means "You're welcome".
7. What does "再见" (zàijiàn) mean?
  • A. Good morning
  • B. Goodbye
  • C. Thank you
  • D. Hello
Correct Answer: B. Goodbye. Literally "again see" (see you again).
8. What is the meaning of "同学" (tóngxué)?
  • A. Teacher
  • B. AI Assistant
  • C. Classmate / Student
  • D. Everyone
Correct Answer: C. Classmate / Student. Teachers often address students as "同学们" (students / class).
9. How do you say "you" in plural form (you all)?
  • A. 您 (nín)
  • B. 你们 (nǐmen)
  • C. 老师 (lǎoshī)
  • D. 大家 (dàjiā)
Correct Answer: B. 你们 (nǐmen). Combining "你" (you) + "们" (plural suffix) creates "你们" (you plural).
10. In Text 3, what does Teacher Wang Yifei say to bid farewell to her students?
  • A. 大家好！
  • B. 不客气！
  • C. 同学们，再见！
  • D. 老师，您好！
Correct Answer: C. 同学们，再见！ ("Goodbye, class! / Goodbye, students!")
