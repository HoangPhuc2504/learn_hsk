Lesson 2
Wǒ jiào Lǐ Wén
我叫李文
My name is Li Wen — Full Review & Quiz
10-Question Review Quiz (复习测验)
1. How do you politely ask someone "May I ask, what is your name?" in Chinese?
  • A. 你是安妮吗？(Nǐ shì Ānnī ma?)
  • B. 请问，你叫什么名字？(Qǐngwèn, nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?)
  • C. 很高兴认识你！(Hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ!)
  • D. 你好，我是李文。(Nǐ hǎo, wǒ shì Lǐ Wén.)
Correct Answer: B. 请问，你叫什么名字？ "请问" (May I ask) is a polite expression used before asking a question like "你叫什么名字？" (What is your name?).
2. What is the standard response to "对不起" (duìbuqǐ - sorry)?
  • A. 不客气 (bú kèqi)
  • B. 没关系 (méi guānxi)
  • C. 我也是 (wǒ yě shì)
  • D. 很高兴 (hěn gāoxìng)
Correct Answer: B. 没关系 (méi guānxi). "没关系" (or colloquial "没事") means "That's all right / No problem" and is used to answer "对不起".
3. What is the basic word order in standard Chinese sentence structure?
  • A. Subject + Object + Verb
  • B. Subject + Predicate/Verb + Object
  • C. Verb + Subject + Object
  • D. Object + Subject + Verb
Correct Answer: B. Subject + Predicate/Verb + Object. As explained in Xiaoyu's Classroom, Chinese follows Subject + Predicate + Object (e.g., 我 [S] 叫 [V] 陈天中 [O]).
4. How do you express "I am not Annie" in Chinese using the negative adverbs?
  • A. 我没安妮。(Wǒ méi Ānnī.)
  • B. 我不是安妮。(Wǒ bù shì Ānnī.)
  • C. 我叫安妮。(Wǒ jiào Ānnī.)
  • D. 我不叫是安妮。(Wǒ bù jiào shì Ānnī.)
Correct Answer: B. 我不是安妮。(Wǒ bù shì Ānnī.) "不" (bù) negates verbs like "是" (shì) to mean "am not / is not".
5. What is the correct structure of a traditional Chinese name?
  • A. Surname + Given Name (e.g., Lǐ + Wén)
  • B. Given Name + Surname (e.g., Wén + Lǐ)
  • C. Middle Name + Surname + Given Name
  • D. Title + Given Name + Surname
Correct Answer: A. Surname + Given Name. Chinese names always put the family surname first, followed by the given name (e.g., 李 [Surname] 文 [Given name]).
6. What does the sentence "认识你我也很高兴" (Rènshi nǐ wǒ yě hěn gāoxìng) mean?
  • A. What is your name?
  • B. I am very happy to meet your teacher.
  • C. Nice to meet you too.
  • D. Sorry, I don't know you.
Correct Answer: C. Nice to meet you too. "也" (yě) means "also / too", making this sentence the standard response to "很高兴认识你".
7. Which character means "also / too" in Chinese?
  • A. 很 (hěn)
  • B. 也 (yě)
  • C. 是 (shì)
  • D. 不 (bù)
Correct Answer: B. 也 (yě). "也" is an adverb meaning "also" or "too".
8. In colloquial spoken Chinese, what is an alternative way to say "没关系" (méi guānxi)?
  • A. 请问 (qǐngwèn)
  • B. 对不起 (duìbuqǐ)
  • C. 没事 / 没事儿 (méishì / méishìr)
  • D. 很高兴 (hěn gāoxìng)
Correct Answer: C. 没事 / 没事儿. As noted in Xiaoyu's Tip, people often say "没事" or "没事儿" in informal spoken Chinese.
9. In the tongue twister from Lesson 2: "七加一，再减一，加完减完等于几？", what is the final answer?
  • A. 六 (6)
  • B. 七 (7)
  • C. 八 (8)
  • D. 一 (1)
Correct Answer: B. 七 (7). "加完减完还是七" (After adding and subtracting, it's still seven).
10. What does the question pronoun "什么" (shénme) mean?
  • A. Who
  • B. Where
  • C. What
  • D. How
Correct Answer: C. What. "什么" is used to ask "what" (e.g., 什么名字 = what name).
